## 1. Breakup Buddy - No Contact
- **Source:** https://apps.apple.com/app/id1667956640 (FETCHED)
- **Onboarding:** Release notes mention an "updated onboarding UI" (v3.2). The flow itself is [unverified].
- **Home / navigation:**
  - Listing mentions a healing profile, "healing globe", dating tab and social feed
  - Tab structure [unverified]
- **Key features:**
  - 24/7 AI chat
  - CBT exercises
  - Journal with healing prompts and analytics
  - Community links
- **Paywall:** Freemium with 2,500 free chat credits, then Pro or token top-ups.
- **Visual identity:** Not described. Release notes mention "profile color updates".
- **Strengths:** Reviewers call the AI empathetic and available 24/7. One says it felt more helpful than their therapist.
- **Weaknesses:**
  - Reviewers complained about AI memory limits (the developer says it doubled the allowance)
  - Confusing token system
  - Feature sprawl (dating tab, social feed)
- **MyEx takeaway:** Compete with a flat subscription and dependable memory for Mira. Stay focused.

## 2. ExDetox
- **Source:** https://apps.apple.com/us/app/exdetox-no-contact-tracker/id6756420232 (FETCHED)
- **Onboarding:** [unverified]. A "My Why" capture is listed as a feature.
- **Home:** A streak counting days, hours and seconds is the core element [layout unverified].

  - Panic Button (write instead of send)
  - AI coach with gentle or blunt modes
  - "Roast Me"
  - Psychology micro-lessons
  - Power Actions that earn points
  - Badges from day 1 to day 90
- **Paywall:** Subscription required for all features. Weekly, monthly and annual plans plus a discounted annual offer.

- **Takeaway:** The direct feature threat. MyEx's edge has to be its premium tone, privacy and the 24-hour lock mechanism.

## 3. NoContact: Breakup Tracker
- **Source:** https://apps.apple.com/app/id6746969199 (FETCHED)
- **Onboarding:** Short quiz on attachment style and breakup timeline.
- **Home:** Streak, confidence meter, badges from bronze to diamond [layout unverified].

  - Emergency Hold-On (60-second breathing animation with haptics and a therapist quote)
  - Learning hub with audio
  - Encrypted journal with mood tracking
  - AI coach and anonymous community [sparse detail]
- **Paywall:** Hard-ish. Core features unlock only with a subscription. Pro adds unlimited emergency sessions and dark mode.
- **Visual identity:** Not described. Dark mode is Pro-only.
- **Strengths:**

- **Weaknesses:**

- **Takeaway:** The SOS screen must stay free and unlimited. Say so in marketing.

## Adjacent benchmarks
- **Finch** (4.9 from about 779K ratings): Penalty-free daily loops and an inclusive companion. Its "subscription shouldn't threaten the companion" principle comes from ScreensDesign's teardown.
- **How We Feel** (4.9 from about 30K ratings, free, nonprofit): Emotion-word check-ins, a "watch it burn" activity and Liquid Glass effects.
- **Rosebud** (4.9 from about 3.4K ratings): AI journaling with memory and weekly analysis. Reported voice-recording loss is its weak point.
- **Ash** (4.8 from about 6.8K ratings): Full-screen, warmer and calmer AI chat, weekly insights and an honest disclaimer.
- **Replika** (4.4 from about 228K ratings): The anti-pattern. Its most prominent complaint is paywalled "hidden" messages.

## Patterns across the category
1. **Nearly everyone has a tracker, panic button and AI coach.** Features alone won't differentiate MyEx.
2. **Visual design is almost never a stated selling point** for breakup apps (none of the 8 fetched listings describes it). Wellness leaders like Stoic, How We Feel and Day One win Editors' Choice partly on design.
3. **Monetisation is aggressive:** weekly plans, rationed emergency sessions, token meters and ads. The opening is an ethical paywall where crisis tools are always free, with one simple tier.
4. **Privacy labels often allow tracking** (ExDetox, NoContact, BREAKFREE, Replika). "No tracking" (as Streaks offers) plus a Face ID lock is a credible differentiator.
5. **Draft-without-sending exists** in Break-Up Boss, ExDetox and BREAKFREE, but **no fetched listing describes a timed 24-hour lock.** Draft Jail is a real distinction [based on the listings fetched, not an exhaustive market check].
